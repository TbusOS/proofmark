The tenth, which renders第十套：它做渲染

hardware-3d is the odd one here. The nine lay out a page; this one renders one — a hand-written WebGL2 engine in a single HTML file with no external dependencies, for explaining how a piece of hardware actually works. Park the camera and every frame re-jitters the sub-pixel offset, the aperture disk, the softbox area and the ambient-occlusion kernel, then averages. Two hundred samples later the soft shadows and the depth of field are sampled rather than approximated. hardware-3d 是这里的另类。那九套是排一张页面，这一套是渲染一张页面 —— 单个 HTML、零外部依赖、手写的 WebGL2 引擎，用来讲一块硬件到底是怎么工作的。相机一停，每帧重新抖动亚像素偏移、光圈盘、柔光箱面积和环境光遮蔽的采样核，然后平均。两百次采样之后，软阴影和景深是采出来的，不是近似出来的。

What the gate checks那道检查查什么

Five checks run in order and stop at the first failure. 五道按顺序跑，第一道不过就停。

Check检查 What it decides它判什么
verify.py Structure: leftover placeholders, missing viewport, undefined classes, BEM modifiers without their base, unbalanced SVG, hero container width, hardcoded colours that already have a token.结构：占位符残留、缺 viewport、未定义的 class、BEM 修饰符缺基类、SVG 标签不配对、hero 容器宽度、已经有 token 却写死的颜色。
visual-audit.mjs The rendered page: contrast, brand presence, diagram label sizes, orphan cards, SVG text legibility, and colour borrowed from another skill's palette.渲染后的页面：对比度、品牌存在感、图表标签字号、孤立卡片、SVG 文字可读性、以及借用了别家调色板的颜色。
axe-audit.mjs Accessibility, via axe-core. Four rules block: color-contrast, link-name, aria-prohibited-attr, svg-img-alt.可访问性，走 axe-core。四条规则阻断：color-contrast、link-name、aria-prohibited-attr、svg-img-alt。
interaction-audit.mjs The states a click reaches: console errors, axe violations that appear only after a click, dead controls, broken in-page anchors, and content revealed onto other content. Every other check judges the first painted frame.点开之后才存在的状态：控制台报错、点击之后才出现的 axe 违规、死控件、断锚点、新露出来压住别的内容。其余每一道判的都是同一个静止画面。
screenshot.mjs A full-page PNG, for the part a person still has to look at.整页 PNG，留给人眼那一部分。

A sixth is opt-in: --pixel compares rendered pixels against a committed baseline. It stays opt-in because an intentional redesign trips it as readily as a regression does. 第六道是选配：--pixel 拿渲染像素跟提交过的基线比。它一直是选配，因为有意的改版和真正的回退一样会让它报错。

What it refuses to judge它不判什么

The gate answers one question: does this page obey the design language it claims to be written in. It says nothing about whether the page is any good, whether the content is correct, or whether a reader will follow the argument. Clearing every check is compatible with the page being worth throwing away. 这道检查只回答一个问题：这一页有没有照着它声称的那套设计语言写。它不回答页面好不好、内容对不对、读者能不能跟上论证。全部检查通过，和这一页该扔掉，两件事可以同时成立。

Contrast ratios, class definitions and label sizes have numeric answers. Whether a layout earns its whitespace does not, and the attempts to mechanise it in earlier rounds produced false positives at a rate that made the check useless. That half is handed to six critic agents, and to a person. 对比度、class 有没有定义、标签多大，这些有数值答案。版面留白是否用得其所没有，早几轮试着把它机械化，误报率高到那条检查没法用。那一半交给六个评审 agent，以及交给人。

How it stops repeating mistakes它怎么做到不重复犯错

Every defect a reviewer catches becomes a row in the known-bugs catalogue, plus a new mechanical check wherever the defect can be detected by machine. 评审员每抓到一个毛病，就变成 known-bugs 目录里的一行；只要机器能判，同时再加一条新检查。
